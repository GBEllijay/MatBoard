## Download window

After the paid Cloud period ends, the gym has 60 days to download its Cloud folder. During those 60 days, sharing between devices through Advantage Cloud is off. After 60 days, Advantage deletes that license's Cloud files. Files already on the gym's phone, or in a folder the gym downloaded, stay with the gym.

Advantage preserves the related records as the law requires, for one year, and removes the content from the gallery. Gallery images are served through a sign-in-gated route on advantagebjjtimer.com so Cloudflare's CSAM Scanning Tool can scan them. A public bucket URL is not used.
