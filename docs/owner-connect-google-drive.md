# Connect your gym folder

Advantage does not host your photos or videos. They stay in a folder the gym already owns. Advantage does not manage that cloud account. You need your own Google, Microsoft, or Apple account. Adding a photo from this phone still uses the gallery. Google Drive is an extra folder.

Coach Unlimited and Advantage Pro stay in alpha until Google Drive, OneDrive, Google Photos, and iCloud all work.

1. Tap **Connect with**.
2. Tap **Google Drive** to sign in and pick a folder. **OneDrive** is the other connect button when this build includes it. OneDrive opens Microsoft in this window, then returns here to pick the folder. **Google Photos** is already included when you pick from gallery (collections come from Google Photos when it is set up). It is not Coming soon. **iCloud** shows as **Coming soon** and is not tappable yet.
3. Sign in with the account that owns the gym folder.
4. For Google, you may see a notice that this connection isn’t verified yet. Tap **Continue**, or **Advanced** then **Continue**, to proceed. This is temporary.
5. Allow Advantage.
6. Choose the gym folder (for example, GBEllijay gallery), or create **Advantage Lesson Plans**.
7. After the folder is connected, **Browse photos and videos** lists folders in that Drive account. Open a folder, tap photos or videos, or tap **Select all**, then **Done**. Those copies stay on this phone for the Media Console gallery. Advantage does not host them.
8. If the card only offers **Reconnect**, the Google sign-in expired or did not include folder listing. Tap **Reconnect** and allow Advantage again. **Disconnect** is still there if you want a different folder.
9. Use **Open my Drive** anytime to open the connected Google Drive folder.
10. A Daily Lesson Plan saved from the app comes back from **Class history**: open the day, or tap **Pick a lesson from Google Drive**. The title, technique notes, and linked video return to the Daily Lesson Plan. Daily Training Videos also has **Pick from Google Drive**.

Lesson plans save in the connected Google Drive folder as a JSON file (`version` 1). Photos and videos stay in the gym’s own account. OneDrive connect writes a small file named `advantage-onedrive-connect.txt` in the folder you pick. Lesson packages on OneDrive, Google Photos, and iCloud are not finished yet.
