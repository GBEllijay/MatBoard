# Connect your gym folder

Advantage does not host your photos or videos. They stay in a folder the gym already owns. Advantage does not manage that cloud account. You need your own Google, Microsoft, or Apple account. Adding a photo from this phone still uses the gallery. Google Drive is an extra folder.

Coach Unlimited and Advantage Pro stay in alpha until Google Drive, OneDrive, Google Photos, and iCloud all work.

1. Tap **Connect with**.
2. Tap **Google Drive** to sign in and pick a folder. **OneDrive** is the other connect button when this build includes it. OneDrive opens Microsoft in this window, then returns here to pick the folder. **Google Photos** connects the gym library. **iCloud** shows as **Coming for launch** and is not tappable yet. They are not optional.
3. Sign in with the account that owns the gym folder.
4. For Google, you may see a notice that this connection isn’t verified yet. Tap **Continue**, or **Advanced** then **Continue**, to proceed. This is temporary.
5. Allow Advantage.
6. Choose the gym folder (for example, GBEllijay gallery), or create **Advantage Lesson Plans**. For **Google Photos**, confirm the library. That step does not pick files.
7. After the folder is connected, **Browse photos and videos** lists folders in that Drive account. Open a folder, tap photos or videos, or tap **Select all**, then **Done**. Those copies stay on this phone for the Media Console gallery. Advantage does not host them.
8. If the card only offers **Reconnect**, the Google sign-in expired or did not include folder listing. Tap **Reconnect** and allow Advantage again. **Change folder** opens the folder list again, or steps up from the current folder, without signing out. **Disconnect Google Drive** asks you to confirm, then forgets the sign-in and the saved folder on this device so you can connect again and pick any folder or account. Lesson plans, rosters, and journals on this device stay. The folder in Google Drive is not deleted.
9. Use **Open my Drive** anytime to open the connected Google Drive folder. **Open Google Photos** opens the connected library.
10. A Daily Lesson Plan saved from the app comes back from **Class history**: open the day, or tap **Pick a lesson from Google Drive**. The title, technique notes, and linked video return to the Daily Lesson Plan. Daily Training Videos also has **Pick from Google Drive**.
11. In **Media Console**, **Pick from Google Photos** opens Google Photos. Search for the album or collection, select the photos or videos, then tap **Done**. **Pick from gallery** is still this phone.

Lesson plans save in the connected Google Drive folder as a JSON file (`version` 1). Photos and videos stay in the gym’s own account. OneDrive connect writes a small file named `advantage-onedrive-connect.txt` in the folder you pick. Lesson packages on OneDrive and iCloud are not finished yet. Google Photos is for picking into Media Console, not for lesson files.
