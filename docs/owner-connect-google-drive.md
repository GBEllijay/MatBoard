# Connect your gym folder

Advantage does not host your photos or videos. They stay in a folder the gym already owns. Adding a photo still uses Google Photos or the phone gallery. Google Drive is an extra folder.

1. Tap **Connect with**.
2. Tap **Google Drive**. OneDrive, Dropbox, and iCloud are in that same list and are coming soon.
3. Sign in with the Google account that owns the gym folder.
4. Google may show a notice that this connection isn’t verified yet. Tap **Continue**, or **Advanced** then **Continue**, to proceed. This is temporary.
5. Allow Advantage.
6. Choose the gallery folder (for example, GBEllijay gallery).
7. Use **Open my Drive** anytime to open that folder.

Lesson plans save in that folder. Photos and videos stay there too.
