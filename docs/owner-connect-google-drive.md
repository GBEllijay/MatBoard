# Connect your gym folder

Advantage does not host your photos or videos. They stay in a folder the gym already owns. Advantage does not manage that cloud account. You need your own Google, Microsoft, or Apple account. Adding a photo from this phone still uses the gallery. Google Drive is an extra folder.

Coach Unlimited and Advantage Pro stay in alpha until Google Drive, OneDrive, Google Photos, and iCloud all work.

1. Tap **Connect with**.
2. Tap **Google Drive**, **OneDrive**, or **iCloud** when that button is available. OneDrive opens Microsoft in this window, then returns here to pick the folder. iCloud opens Apple in this window, then returns here. **Google Photos** shows as **Coming for launch** until it works. These four are not optional.
3. Sign in with the account that owns the gym folder.
4. For Google, you may see a notice that this connection isn’t verified yet. Tap **Continue**, or **Advanced** then **Continue**, to proceed. This is temporary.
5. Allow Advantage.
6. Choose the gym folder (for example, GBEllijay gallery), or create **Advantage Lesson Plans**.
7. Use **Open my Drive** anytime to open the connected Google Drive folder.

Lesson plans save in the connected Google Drive folder. Photos and videos stay in the gym’s own account. OneDrive connect writes a small file named `advantage-onedrive-connect.txt` in the folder you pick. iCloud connect writes a small note named `advantage-icloud-connect.txt` in the gym’s iCloud. That note does not show up as an iCloud Drive file. Lesson packages on OneDrive, Google Photos, and iCloud are not finished yet.
